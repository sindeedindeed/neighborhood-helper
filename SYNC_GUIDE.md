## 🚀 SYNC NOW - Follow These Steps

### Step 1: Click File Menu
- Look at the top of Android Studio
- Click **File**

### Step 2: Click Sync Now
- In the dropdown menu, click **Sync Now**
- You'll see a notification at the bottom of the screen

### Step 3: Wait for Sync to Complete
- Android Studio will download dependencies
- This takes 2-3 minutes on first sync
- You'll see a progress bar at the bottom
- Wait until it says "Gradle sync finished" ✅

### Step 4: After Sync Completes
- The green **▶ Run** button will appear in top-right
- If it doesn't appear, click **Build → Make Project**

---

## 🎯 After Sync

### Option A: Click Run Button
1. Look for the green **▶ Run** button in top-right corner
2. Click it
3. Select your emulator or device
4. App launches! 🎉

### Option B: Use Keyboard Shortcut
1. Press **Shift + F10** to run
2. Select your emulator or device
3. App launches! 🎉
